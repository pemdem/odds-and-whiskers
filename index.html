Pick your computer

On Windows you need Python 3.12, 3.13 or 3.14 (free; the installer checks and opens the download page if it's missing). Tutorial

Getting started

  1. Get Python 3.14

    On python.org, download the Windows installer (64-bit) for Python 3.14 (3.12 and 3.13 work too; 3.15 doesn't yet). When it opens, tick Add python.exe to PATH and click Install Now. Already have one of those? Skip this — the installer finds it.

    python.org/downloads/windows

  2. Unzip the package

    Right-click OddsAndWhiskers-1.11.0-windows.zip → Extract All… → pick a place like Documents. You get a folder called Odds and Whiskers. Keep it there; the app lives in it.

  3. Run the installer

    Open the folder and double-click Install Odds and Whiskers.bat. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway. It takes a minute or two.

  4. Open it and practise

    Double-click the new Odds & Whiskers icon on your Desktop. It starts in practice mode: fake money on real Kalshi prices. Look around: Cat Corner, the Litter Box, Backtrack, the Clump slider. Hover anything to see what it means.

  5. Connect your Kalshi account (real money only)

    On kalshi.com, open your account settings → API keys → create a key. The app’s welcome (or Settings → Kalshi account) asks for the Key ID and the key file. It’s stored only on your PC, encrypted and locked by Windows Credential Manager.

  6. Move money and go live

    Still in the app: move money into Kalshi’s crypto section if needed, then choose the most the bot may use and a risk level (start at Tiny), and type the confirmation phrase yourself. The app reopens in real-money mode. The confirmation lasts 30 days.

  1. Open Odds & Whiskers Setup

    Open the download (Safari unzips it for you) and double-click Odds & Whiskers Setup. No Terminal, and no separate Python install: Setup brings its own.

  2. Allow it once

    macOS will probably say Apple can’t check it for malicious software, because it isn’t signed with a paid Apple certificate. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway next to “Odds & Whiskers Setup”, then confirm with your password. You only do this once.

  3. Click Install

    Setup copies the app, downloads its own Python (checked before use) and installs everything by itself, in 2 to 3 minutes. Odds & Whiskers then opens and appears on your Desktop and in Applications.

  4. Follow the welcome

    It starts in practice mode: fake money on real Kalshi prices. The welcome walks you through the rest, all inside the app. Keep the Mac plugged in and awake while it trades; it can’t bet while the Mac sleeps.

  5. Connect your Kalshi account (real money only)

    On kalshi.com, open your account settings → API keys → create a key. In the app, paste the Key ID and choose the key file. It’s stored only on your Mac, encrypted and locked by your Keychain.

  6. Move money and go live

    Still in the app: move money into Kalshi’s crypto section if needed, then choose the most the bot may use and a risk level (start at Tiny), and type the confirmation phrase yourself. The app reopens in real-money mode.

Real money is your call. This strategy has not been shown to be profitable. A win pays a few cents per contract and a loss costs the whole price. Start small, and use practice mode and the Litter Box first.
Your setup stays yours

One app, separate accounts

On your computer only

Your Kalshi key, limits, settings, layouts, trade history and Litter Box results never leave your computer and are never replaced by an update.

Learns from you

Strategy recommendations come from your own results. Nobody's account or bets are shared — only the app itself is.

Same updates everywhere

Windows and Mac copies check this website for new versions. Every update is signed, so the app only installs updates from the Odds & Whiskers publisher.

Getting updates

Nothing to set up. The app checks this website for new versions twice an hour. When one is out, it shows Update available. Click Install and it does this by itself:

New bets pause→Waiting bids are cancelled→ Your history and settings are backed up→The update installs→Every order is re-checked with Kalshi→Betting resumes

Bets that already filled are never touched. After each new version, a What's new popup shows once, and Settings → Updates keeps the full history.
